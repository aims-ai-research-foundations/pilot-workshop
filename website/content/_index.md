The **AIMS Pilot Workshop** is an internal dry run of the lecturer workshops as part of the AI Research Foundations for Higher Education programme.

After a month of virtual onboarding working through the AI Research Foundations courses created by Google DeepMind, you'll spend one week in person at AIMS South Africa strengthening your knowledge of cutting edge AI topics, collaborating with researchers, sharpening your teaching craft, and helping us refine the workshop before it rolls out across the continent.
