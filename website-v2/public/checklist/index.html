What to bring & what we provide

What to bring

  • Laptop and charger
  • South African power adaptor
  • Jacket (Muizenberg winters are cold)
  • Notebook and pen

What we provide

  • Airport transfers between Cape Town International airport and AIMS
  • Comfortable accommodation, walking distance from Muizenberg beach
  • Three meals a day
  • Tea and coffee during breaks
  • 24/7 Wi-Fi access at up to 1Gb per second (fast!)

Dietary or accessibility needs?

Email ai-research-foundations@aims.ac.za and we'll sort it out.
