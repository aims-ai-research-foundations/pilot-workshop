## About

Train a small neural network on a noisy 2-D classification problem and **see
overfitting with your own eyes**. You then design and run two experiments to
fight it — reducing the network's capacity, and adding regularisation — and
relate model complexity to bias and variance. About 60 minutes; no GPU needed.
