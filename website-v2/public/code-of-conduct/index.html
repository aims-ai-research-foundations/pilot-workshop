Code of Conduct

We are committed to creating a safe, inclusive, and respectful environment where everyone can learn, share, and contribute. These four principles guide how we work together during the workshop.

Respect others

Treat everyone with dignity and respect. We do not tolerate discrimination or harassment of any kind.

Be present

Stay engaged, participate fully, and minimise distractions.

Be constructive

Share ideas and feedback in a way that is helpful and solution-focused.

Own the experience

Take responsibility for your experience and help create a positive environment for everyone.

We all have a role to play.

If you experience or witness behaviour that goes against this Code of Conduct, please speak with a member of the workshop team. We will address concerns promptly and respectfully, or email ai-research-foundations@aims.ac.za.

Questions about the workshop?

We're here to support you before, during, and after.
